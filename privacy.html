Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026  ·  Effective: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quenmark Solutions ("we", "us", or "our"), based in Virar, India, collects, uses, and protects information you provide when using our website (quenmarksolutions.com) and our services. By accessing or using our site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you contact us, request a quote, or fill out any form on our website, we may collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Company or brand name
  • Project details and requirements you share with us

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain technical data, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages viewed and time spent on our site
  • Referring website or link

This data is collected through standard web server logs and may be aggregated and anonymised. We do not use invasive tracking or third-party advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries — When you reach out via our contact form, we use your name and email to reply to your message.
  • To provide our services — If you engage us for a project, we use your information to deliver, manage, and communicate about the work.
  • To improve our website — We analyse aggregated traffic data to understand how visitors use our site and to improve our content and performance.
  • To send important communications — We may send you project updates, invoices, or service-related notices. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party tools such as FormSubmit to process contact form submissions. These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and only receive the data necessary to perform their function.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or restructuring of Quenmark Solutions, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you in advance.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Contact form submissions are typically retained for up to 2 years. Project-related communications may be retained for up to 5 years for record-keeping purposes.

5. Cookies

Our website uses only essential, functional cookies — specifically, a single cookie to remember your preferred theme (dark or light mode). This cookie stores no personally identifiable information, does not track you across other websites, and does not expire until you clear your browser storage. We do not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: If you consented to any data processing, you may withdraw that consent at any time.
  • Right to Object: Object to any processing of your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at quenmarksolutions@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include using HTTPS encryption across our website and limiting access to data to only those team members who need it. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use caution when sharing sensitive information online.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites, including the live projects we have developed for clients. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
